Research

I study theoretical methods for controlling and verifying the quantum states of mechanical oscillators using light. My current interests include conditional-state estimation from continuous measurements and quantum filtering, momentum squeezing of macroscopic mirrors, and strategies for detecting gravity-induced entanglement. I am also interested in tests of quantum foundations with harmonic oscillators.

For an overview of my work and the most recent publications, see the papers below and my Google Scholar profile.
